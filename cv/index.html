Professional Summary

PhD candidate at Stevens Institute of Technology developing computer vision and deep learning methods for robotic perception in visually degraded environments, with underwater infrastructure inspection as the main application. Experienced in semantic segmentation, underwater image enhancement, few-shot learning, and efficient model deployment through structured pruning, with first-author work in Automation in Construction and ASCE i3CE, and a Runner-up Paper Award at IConIC 2026.

Experience

  • 2023 - Present

    Hoboken, NJ

    Research Assistant
    Stevens Institute of Technology
    Underwater vision and robotics for automated infrastructure inspection and maintenance
    • Developed a PyTorch-based semantic segmentation method to localize bridge components in underwater inspection images, achieving an IoU of 0.88.
    • Proposed a domain-specific encoder that learns image representations from quality-specific feature maps to address underwater image degradation.
    • Built a PyTorch pipeline integrating image enhancement, classification, and semantic segmentation for underwater structure detection.
    • Developed a few-shot semantic segmentation method using cross-attention between query images and support-image regions of interest to detect seven common damage types on underwater bridge components.
    • Built ontology-driven reasoning and action-planning tools to support autonomous underwater bridge inspection and maintenance decisions.
